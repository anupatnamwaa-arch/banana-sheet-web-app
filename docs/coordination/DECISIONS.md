# Nana Daily Brief Decisions

Approved product decisions for phase one:

- Banana Sheet remains the product name. Nana is the in-app companion and coaching voice.
- Home is adaptive and shows no more than three visible decisions before scrolling.
- Safe to Spend is the primary answer. Banana Money Score is secondary.
- Rules determine financial meaning and priority. AI enrichment is optional and non-blocking.
- Home launches with curated Thai-first Nana copy.
- Daily Brief refreshes each morning and after meaningful events, not after every Transaction.
- Meaningful events include important Income, unusual Expense, Category threshold crossing, pace-state change, and upcoming Fixed Expense pressure.
- Safe to Spend protects upcoming Fixed Expenses and committed saving before dividing flexible money across days remaining.
- Use configured billing cycle when available, otherwise calendar month.
- If current-cycle Income is missing, use a labeled previous-cycle estimate when history exists; never silently use stock metrics.
- Clamp negative Safe to Spend to `฿0/day` and explain the shortfall in recovery detail.
- Store one evolving private Daily Brief per user per Bangkok date. Keep 30 days for future reflection; no history screen in phase one.
- The hero uses a stable everyday skeleton with takeover variants only for payday and setup.
- Payday phase one offers `ตั้งเป้าเงินออม` and `ไว้ทีหลัง`; full allocation is deferred.
- Suggestions are plain guidance and may be hidden for the current day.
- Keep Analytics unchanged in phase one.
